## 💡 Usage Tips

- **Keep quotes concise** (2-3 sentences max for readability)
- **Use real-looking names and companies** (increases credibility)
- **Include specific results** when possible (percentages, timeframes)
- **Vary the roles** (managers, individual contributors, executives)
- **Always include avatars** (stock photos or professional headshots)
